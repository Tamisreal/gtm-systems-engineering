# Zero to one: Amazon FBA wholesale business

I started and ran an Amazon FBA wholesale business that grossed about $3M a year: buying branded products from distributors and manufacturers and selling them through Amazon's fulfillment network.

## Why it belongs here

It is the first thing I built from zero: no playbook, real revenue and operations that had to work every day. Wholesale is a sales and operations problem before it is a technology one. Opening supplier accounts is an outbound motion, product selection is a data problem, and margin depends on unit economics, not volume. That is the same order of thinking the rest of this repository applies to GTM systems.
