# Zero to one: Amazon FBA wholesale business

I started and ran an Amazon FBA (Fulfilled by Amazon) wholesale business that grossed about $3M a year. I bought branded products from distributors and manufacturers and sold them on Amazon, with Amazon storing and shipping the orders.

## Why it belongs here

It is the first thing I built from zero. There was no playbook, and the revenue and operations had to work every day. Wholesale is a sales and operations problem before it is a technology one. Opening supplier accounts is outbound selling. Picking products is a data problem. Profit depends on the margin on each unit, not on volume. The rest of this repository applies the same order of thinking to GTM (go-to-market) systems.
