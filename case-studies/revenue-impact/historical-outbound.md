They have not been checked against CRM exports, the source of each number is not yet recorded, and **none of them is attributed to AI or agents**. They are listed so they can be verified, not so they can be quoted.

## How each figure should be documented once verified

Each figure moves to [IMPACT.md](../../IMPACT.md) only with a complete record from the [impact record template](../../templates/impact-record-template.md):

- business problem and baseline
- intervention, and the role AI or automation played, if any
- measurement period and source system
- metric definition and formula
- attribution method: **sourced** (the motion created the opportunity) versus **influenced** (the motion touched an opportunity created another way); never summed
- confounding factors (seasonality, territory changes, other campaigns, rep changes)
- limitations
- the business decision taken because of the result

## What a strong version of this page would show

For the outbound program: the funnel by stage with each conversion rate, cost per qualified meeting (tool spend, data spend and labor divided by qualified meetings), and the comparison that makes it a result instead of a number: the same metric for the period before, or for a holdout group.
