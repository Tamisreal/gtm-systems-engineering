# Evidence checklist

Every historical claim in this repository is listed here with what it needs before it can be labeled **Measured / Verified**. Evidence itself (CRM exports, screenshots, statements) stays private. Only the verified figure, its definition and its method are published.

Rule: if a source cannot be produced, the figure stays **Historical / Pending Verification** or is removed. It is never rounded up, combined with another figure, or attributed to AI without a method that isolates the AI's contribution.
