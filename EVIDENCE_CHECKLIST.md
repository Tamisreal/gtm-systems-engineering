# Evidence checklist

Every historical claim in this repository is listed here with what it needs before it can be labeled **Measured / Verified**. Evidence itself (CRM exports, screenshots, statements) stays private. Only the verified figure, its definition and its method are published.

Rule: if a source cannot be produced, the figure stays **Historical / Pending Verification** or is removed. It is never rounded up, combined with another figure, or attributed to AI without a method that isolates the AI's contribution.

## Outbound results (69 meetings, 27 opportunities, about $436,834 pipeline ARR)

- [ ] Which company and motion produced these, and under what role?
- [ ] Exact date range for "five months".
- [ ] Meeting definition: booked or held? Source report?
- [ ] Opportunity definition: created by whom, at what stage?
- [ ] Pipeline ARR: sourced or influenced? Which amount field, measured on what date, at what stages?
- [ ] Any closed-won revenue from these opportunities?
- [ ] Were AI or automated steps part of the workflow? Which ones, and is there any comparison (before/after, holdout) that isolates their effect?
- [ ] Permission to name the employer or client, or a description that does not identify them.

## ICP and experiment figures (589 accounts, about 8,000 contacts, about 4% reply, about 40% positive, about 30% to pipeline)

- [ ] ICP definition used to create the 589 accounts.
- [ ] Is the 8,000-contact experiment the same motion as the 69 meetings above, or a different one?
- [ ] Reply rate denominator: sent or delivered? Auto-replies and out-of-office excluded?
- [ ] Who classified positive replies, and by what rule?
- [ ] Definition of "converted to pipeline" and the time window.
- [ ] Sequencer or CRM export covering the period.
